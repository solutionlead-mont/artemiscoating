What type of flooring are you looking for?

Hardwood
Solid wood

Real solid wood — timeless, warm, refinishable for decades.

Pros

  • Adds the most resale value
  • Can be sanded & refinished multiple times
  • Lasts 50+ years if maintained

Cons

  • Sensitive to moisture — not for bathrooms/basements
  • Higher cost, longer install

Popular colors: Natural Oak, Espresso / Dark Walnut, Grey-washed

Engineered Wood
Multi-ply

Real wood top layer over a stable core — the look of hardwood, more forgiving.

Pros

  • Real wood surface, lower price than solid
  • Handles humidity better than solid wood
  • Can go over concrete slabs

Cons

  • Only refinishable once or twice
  • Cheaper versions wear faster

Popular colors: Light Oak, Natural Hickory, Weathered Grey

LVP / SPC
Vinyl plank

Waterproof, durable, budget-friendly — today's most popular floor.

Pros

  • 100% waterproof — great for any room
  • Scratch & kid/pet resistant
  • Easy, fast install

Cons

  • Won't add resale value like real wood
  • Can dent under very heavy furniture

Popular colors: Grey Oak, Warm Honey, Weathered / Rustic Brown

Laminate
Click-lock

Wood-look at the lowest price point — tough surface, easy upkeep.

Pros

  • Very affordable
  • Scratch-resistant, low maintenance
  • Wide range of looks

Cons

  • Not fully waterproof unless rated
  • Can't be refinished — replace when worn

Popular colors: Golden Oak, Light Maple, Grey

Ceramic / Porcelain
Tile

Hard, waterproof, virtually indestructible — ideal for wet areas.

Pros

  • Fully waterproof & stain-proof
  • Extremely durable, lasts decades
  • Great for kitchens, baths, entryways

Cons

  • Hard & cold underfoot
  • Grout needs occasional sealing

Popular colors: White / Marble-look, Grey Stone-look, Beige / Sand

Natural Stone
Marble, travertine

Premium, one-of-a-kind natural material — luxury underfoot.

Pros

  • High-end look, unique in every slab
  • Adds strong resale value
  • Extremely long-lasting

Cons

  • Needs sealing & special care
  • Highest price point

Popular colors: White Marble, Beige Travertine, Dark Slate

Carpet
Soft & warm

Soft, warm, and quiet — comfort where you want it most.

Pros

  • Warm and comfortable underfoot
  • Reduces noise
  • Most budget-friendly

Cons

  • Stains & wears faster than hard floors
  • Not ideal for allergies or high-moisture areas

Popular colors: Beige / Greige, Grey, Warm Taupe

Concrete & Epoxy
Garage, basement, patio

Seamless, ultra-tough coating — built for garages, shops, and modern spaces.

Pros

  • Extremely durable — resists stains, chemicals, impact
  • Seamless, easy to clean
  • Custom colors & finishes

Cons

  • Requires solid concrete base
  • Hard surface, install needs cure time

Popular colors: Grey, Tan / Beige, Blue-black Metallic

Polished Concrete
Polished slab

Grind and polish your existing slab into a sleek, low-maintenance floor.

Pros

  • Uses your existing slab — no new material
  • Ultra durable, near-zero maintenance
  • Modern, high-end industrial look

Cons

  • Hard & cold underfoot
  • Slab must be in decent shape

Popular colors: Natural Grey, Charcoal, Warm Tan
